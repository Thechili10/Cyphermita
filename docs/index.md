# Welcome to Cyphermita 🛡️

> **LEARN • EXPLORE • PRACTICE • BUILD**

**Cyphermita** is an open-source cybersecurity learning roadmap designed to take you from computer fundamentals to career readiness. 

---

### 💡 Our Philosophy
Don't just learn what tools to use. Learn **what** to learn, **why** it matters, **when** to use those tools, and **how** to solve complex problems.
